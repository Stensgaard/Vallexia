# Vallexia - Smart Meal Planning App

> Simplify meal planning, reduce food waste, and achieve your nutrition goals with an intelligent meal planning application.

Vallexia helps you create personalized meal plans that fit your lifestyle, dietary needs, and nutritional goals. Whether you're planning meals for yourself or your entire household, Vallexia makes it easy to stay organized, eat healthily, and save time in the kitchen.

## What is Vallexia?

Vallexia is a comprehensive meal planning platform designed to take the stress out of meal preparation. Instead of wondering "what's for dinner?" every day, Vallexia helps you plan ahead, shop smart, and stay on track with your health goals.

### Key Benefits

- 🍽️ **Save Time** - Plan your meals in advance and eliminate daily decision fatigue
- 🛒 **Reduce Food Waste** - Generate precise shopping lists based on your planned meals
- 🎯 **Meet Your Goals** - Track nutrition and stay aligned with your dietary targets
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Family-Friendly** - Plan meals for any household size with shared preferences
- 🥗 **Dietary Flexibility** - Support for all dietary restrictions, allergies, and preferences

## Features

### User Profiles & Preferences
Create a personalized profile that remembers your preferences, dietary restrictions, and nutritional goals. Set your household size, meal types (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks), and let Vallexia tailor recommendations to your needs.

### Dietary Management
- **Restrictions** - Mark dietary restrictions (vegetarian, vegan, keto, etc.)
- **Allergies** - Track food allergies to ensure safe meal suggestions
- **Cuisine Preferences** - Indicate preferred cuisines and flavors
- **Dislikes** - Avoid specific ingredients you don't enjoy

### Nutritional Goals
Set and track your daily nutritional targets:
- **Calories** - Daily calorie goals
- **Macronutrients** - Protein, carbohydrates, and fats
- **Micronutrients** - Fiber, sodium, sugar, and more
- **Goal Types** - Weight maintenance, loss, or gain

### Meal Planning *(Coming Soon)*
Plan your meals with an intuitive calendar interface:
- **Weekly Planning** - See your entire week at a glance
- **Drag & Drop** - Easily assign meals to different days
- **Multiple Meals** - Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks
- **Meal Templates** - Save and reuse your favorite meal combinations

### Recipe Management *(Coming Soon)*
Build your personal recipe collection:
- **Recipe Library** - Store all your favorite recipes in one place
- **Ingredient Lists** - Detailed ingredient lists with quantities
- **Nutritional Information** - Automatic calculation of nutritional values
- **Search & Filter** - Find recipes by ingredients, categories, or dietary restrictions
- **Rating & Reviews** - Rate recipes and add personal notes

### Grocery Lists *(Coming Soon)*
Streamline your shopping:
- **Auto-Generation** - Automatically create shopping lists from your meal plans
- **Smart Consolidation** - Combine similar ingredients across multiple recipes
- **Quantity Calculations** - Get exact amounts needed based on household size
- **Store Organization** - Lists organized by category or store layout

### Nutritional Tracking *(Coming Soon)*
Monitor your progress:
- **Daily Intake** - Track what you actually ate versus your goals
- **Weekly Overview** - See nutritional patterns across the week
- **Goal Progress** - Visual indicators showing progress toward your targets
- **Recommendations** - Suggestions to adjust meals and meet your goals

### Smart Recommendations *(Coming Soon)*
Get personalized meal suggestions:
- **AI-Powered Suggestions** - Recipes matched to your preferences and goals
- **Seasonal Ingredients** - Recommendations based on what's in season
- **Nutritional Balance** - Suggestions to round out your meal plan
- **Learning System** - Gets smarter as you use it more
